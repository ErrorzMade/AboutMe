[PhasOverlay Web ](phasoverlay.xyz)is a powerful evidence tracker for Phasmophobia. It has features such as evidence tracking, detailed ghost information, timers and has a feature that allows you to tap to the speed of a ghost to work out it's speed. A few extra features it also has is Link, which is a system that lets you sync up PhasOverlay with up to 7 other people. It also features a regularly updated weekly challenge preset, updated every Monday (usually within a few hours of the weekly changing).
